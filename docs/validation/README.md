# Validation kit: will controllers pay?

The plan says the build only goes further if a validation test passes. The MVP now exists, so it becomes the
demo for that test. This folder has what you need for two weeks of conversations:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `outreach-ro.md` | First message (LinkedIn / email) and a follow-up, in Romanian |
| `interview-script-ro.md` | 30-minute conversation: problems first, demo second, commitment last (Romanian) |
| `demo-walkthrough.md` | The 10-minute demo on the test pack: what to click and what to say |
| `tracker.csv` | One row per conversation; open in Excel |

## Who to talk to

- **7–10 companies:** CFOs, finance controllers or chief accountants at firms with 50–250 employees in
  supplier-heavy sectors (distribution, construction, manufacturing, retail chains).
- **3 accounting firms** that keep books for companies of that size (the channel in the plan).
- Sources: your own network (outside work), LinkedIn searches by title + sector + company size, CECCAR members,
  the Bucharest chamber of commerce (CCIB), and asking every person at the end for one introduction.
- **Do not approach Siemens Energy, its subsidiaries or its suppliers**, and do this on your own time and
  equipment (see the founder check in the plan).

## Proposed go / no-go (confirm or change before the first call)

The plan names a success threshold but no numbers. A proposal:

| Result after 2 weeks | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥ 10 conversations, **≥ 3 commit to a pilot on their real archive with a date**, and ≥ 3 describe a costly incident (duplicate paid, missed price increase, VAT correction, payment fraud attempt) from the last 2 years | **Go:** run the pilots, then price |
| 1–2 pilot commitments, or interest only from accounting firms | **Adjust:** test the accounting-firm offer or a different segment for 2 more weeks |
| No pilot commitment and few incidents | **Stop or pivot:** the pain is not strong enough at this size |

A "commitment" means a date and an agreement to run the tool on their own machine, ideally paid (even a
symbolic fee). "Interesting, send me something" does not count.

## Rules during validation

- **Never take invoices away.** Demos use the synthetic test pack. Pilots run on the client's computer, the
  client exports the report, and you keep only what they choose to share (e.g. the anonymised summary page).
- Do not give tax or accounting opinions about their cases; ask how they handled them.
- Write notes into `tracker.csv` the same day. Record numbers they give (amounts, invoice volumes, prices), not
  your impressions.
